# Project Brief: Luxury Watch Website

## Overview
A high-end, cinematic digital product film (website) showcasing a luxury watch's anatomy, disassembly, and precision engineering. The site operates as a continuous, scroll-hijacked visual experience.

## Core Requirements
- **Performance:** Maintain 60fps frame-by-frame canvas rendering across 12 video sequences (2880 frames total).
- **Aesthetics:** "Exaggerated Minimalism," Awwwards-worthy design, rich gold accents (`#c9a84c`), and premium typography (Inter & Playfair Display).
- **Interactivity:** Advanced scroll-linked animations, char-by-char blur reveals, magnetic buttons, and an autoplay feature.

## Goals
- Serve as a showcase of "Creative Engineering" for Shantanu's portfolio.
- Deliver an Apple/Rolex-tier user experience with seamless transitions.
- Keep the user engaged with micro-interactions (stat counters, floating annotations, film grain).
